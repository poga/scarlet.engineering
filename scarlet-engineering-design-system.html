scarlet.engineering
design system v2.1 · cold world, punk print, hot people

the world does not care. the characters answer that with attitude. distance lives in the landscape, the palette, and the narration. punk lives in the materials, the type, and the characters. backgrounds are never punk. characters are never cold.

where scarlet goes

  • the mark and the light source.
  • characters: a scarf, a blade, a stain.
  • one scarlet subject per composition. a character may carry several red marks if they read as one gesture.

where it never goes

  • the landscape. sky, ground, rock, and water stay basalt, slate, bone.
  • a second red. punk comes from contrast and misregistration, not from adding orange.

four colors, under any circumstances. no tints, no shades, no opacity ramps. texture is made of dots, not of alpha. store brand colors (steam, itch) do not appear in studio compositions; store links are bone outline buttons.

04mark

a scarlet disc over a black structure. the structure is a mast: flat black, rough edge, no detail. the disc sits at the top or hangs behind it as a halo drawn in concentric bands. it prints as two colors on bone paper.

large · 320px · halftone rings, grain, torn edge
medium · 96px · mast, disc, one ring
small · 24px · black vertical, red circle

reverse on basalt (favicon, ui): the vertical prints in bone. the disc stays scarlet.

the mark is the only place scarlet may be a shape larger than a period. everywhere else it is a dot, a rule, or one button.

05rules

composition

world shots

  • landscape, structures, the mark.
  • low horizon or none.
  • subject small or monolithic, never medium.
  • large empty field.
  • one light source, drawn as a flat disc or concentric bands.
  • prefer vertical and square formats.

character shots

  • close and cropped. head and shoulders, or a single hand and object.
  • cut off by the frame edge.
  • high contrast, flat black shapes.
  • no background, or a single flat field.
  • never a full-body medium shot in a landscape. that reads as neither cold nor punk.

combined shots

  • character silhouette in the foreground, cropped and large.
  • world small and far behind.
  • the character is the only thing with detail.

texture

print damage, encouraged

  • ink spatter and drips.
  • stencil bridges and overspray.
  • tape, staples, torn edges, folded-paper creases.
  • misregistration up to 2 to 3px between black and scarlet layers.
  • photocopy contrast crush on characters: pure black, pure bone, no midtone.
  • all texture in black or scarlet only.

the paper test

  • damage must look like it happened to paper.
  • no gradients, drop shadows, glow, chromatic aberration.
  • no digital noise filters.
  • motion, if any: hard cuts, stamped frames, registration jitter. scarlet lands last.

faces

characters

  • faces are allowed.
  • eyes may be hidden by hair, hood, mask, shadow, goggles. they do not have to be.
  • masks and partial coverings are preferred where the choice is free.
  • expressions stay minimal. angry, tired, or amused. never surprised, never crying.

the world

  • no faces on the world. structures, weather, and animals stay silhouettes.

print logic

one duplicator

  • everything is two-color riso: black plus scarlet on bone paper.
  • site, steam banner, business card come off the same duplicator.
  • the page is basalt. bone is for type and paper elements, not the page.
  • one scarlet subject per composition. count before you ship.

avoid

  • orange, ochre, warm browns, gold.
  • gradients, glow, rays, lens flare, drop shadows, chromatic aberration.
  • smooth vector illustration.
  • faces on the world. emotional expressions on characters.
  • full-body characters standing in landscapes.
  • characters in slate or bone with no black.
  • distressed mono text.
  • scarlet on sky, ground, or water.
  • more than one red per frame. more than four colors under any circumstances.
  • easing and fades.
  • detective or victorian references.

copy voice

two voices. the narration never adopts the dialogue voice. the dialogue never adopts the narration voice.

narration · studio text, ui, world · mono · saga register

the pass closed in the ninth week. we burned the sledge for heat. the tower stands.

short declaratives. concrete nouns. no adjectives that tell the reader how to feel. lowercase. never describes what a character feels.

dialogue and slogans · characters, posters · display or mono · punk register
i didn't come for the view.

may be funny, may be rude, may be in caps. still short. the only place a character gets to talk back.

// narration, yes the land was here first. we build in the cold. // dialogue, yes NOBODY IS COMING. GOOD. // no, either voice an epic journey awaits! she felt a deep sorrow as the snow fell.

06characters

cold world, hot people. every character is built from four parts. this is a hard template, not a suggestion. characters are stated, not explored. narration never describes what they feel. everything is shown through the object and the rule.

  1. silhouette. readable in solid black at 32px. scavenged, wrong-sized, spiky, or asymmetric. built from found parts.
  2. rule. one thing they will not do, or will always do, stated in under eight words.
  3. object. one tool or weapon they made or took. it appears in every depiction.
  4. contradiction. the thing that makes the rule cost them something. may be funny.
